          NOT DAMAGED / DAMAGED / DESTROYED

          1 / 16

          In my dreams, I try to warn people of an inevitable disaster, but they never believe me. When I wake up, I can never remember my message, only the sensation of impending doom.

          I think about how the world ends, not in fire or water, but in ash, dust, and silence. When I imagine the end, it is after the dust has settled. I walk through the streets and everything is familiar, but I can't orient myself. It's quiet. I think, "There are supposed to be bells ringing when the world is ending."

          And yet, the end has already come and passed, and I am still here.

          Installation dimensions:

          • Room: 17′ 3″ × 20′
          • Boardwalk: L-shaped wooden structure, 17′ overall length; 12′ × 5′ upper section; 12′ lower section; 3′ 3″ wide; 8′ 6″ interior span
          • House: 32″ × 32″ × 12′ (height)
          • Materials: Wood, rubber, found, appropriated, and purchased objects
